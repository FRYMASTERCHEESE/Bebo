Bebo Is Officially Back ❤️

A new, independent community inspired by classic Bebo. This website is a fan-made social networking project celebrating the colourful personal profiles and social features people loved in the 2000s.

It is more than a static replica: it has a real connected backend built with Supabase for member accounts and saved data. GitHub Pages hosts the website interface; Supabase provides authentication and database services.

Independent project notice: This community is not the original Bebo service, not affiliated with its founders or former corporate owners, and not an official restoration of old Bebo accounts. The words “Bebo Is Officially Back” are this independent project's slogan, not a claim of endorsement, ownership or affiliation.
Explore the new Bebo ♥

Real accounts and saved profiles

Members can register with email, sign in and maintain a personal profile. Account and profile data is stored in the dedicated Supabase backend rather than being reset when a browser tab closes.

Profiles can include a display name, personal information, avatar and a custom look.

Classic-style social features

  • Friend requests, Top 16 friends and Daily Luv
  • Profile wall comments and whiteboards
  • Private messages, photo albums and blogs
  • Groups, Other Half, polls, quizzes and creator pages

56 original skins

Browse 56 newly created profile skin designs inspired by 2005–2007 internet culture, with classic, colourful, music, glitter, dark and other styles. Members can choose a preset or use custom styling tools.

These are original interpretations rather than recovered copyrighted skins from the historical Bebo platform.

Safety, privacy and current status

Bebo provides community guidelines and tools for blocking, reporting and account management. Some operations still require full real-user acceptance and security testing, so the site should be treated as a developing independent community.

Read Safety & Privacy before joining. Do not reuse passwords from old or unrelated websites.

Advertising, rules and privacy

We are preparing for a possible advertising application; Google ad scripts have not been enabled as part of this work. Member-created media must comply with community rules. Videos and new album photos need moderator approval before public listing; wall/profile reports are reviewed by the authorised admin team.

Can I access my old Bebo account?

No. Accounts and content from the historical Bebo service are not imported or recovered by this project. Members create fresh accounts on this new independent community.

Bebo Is Officially Back refers to the community revival experience, not a restoration of the original company or database.
