How to use Bebo.nz ♥

Welcome to a new, independent community inspired by classic Bebo. You don't need to remember your old Bebo password: this is a new website with new accounts. Here's how to find your feet and have fun with a more colourful profile.

1. Choose your classic Bebo era

At the top of the website, switch between 2005 Classic and 2007 Classic. The earlier look uses a lighter blue, white and lilac style; the later look brings back richer colours and classic social modules. The switch changes your browser's appearance, not the underlying friends or posts. You can change your mind whenever you like.

2. Create an account — it's free

Select Sign Up — Free at the top. Use an email address you can open, and a password you have never used on another site. The sign-up form asks you to confirm that you are at least 18 years old and agree to the community rules. Check your inbox for the verification email. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder and confirm you entered the correct email address.

Already registered? Use Sign In. If you've forgotten a password, open Sign In and choose Forgot password?. Do not share the reset link or verification codes with anyone.

3. Build a profile that feels like yours

Once signed in, choose your Bebo name, display name, status and a little About Me text. You can add a profile picture and pick one of the site's original styles. Keep real addresses, phone numbers and other private details off your public profile; other visitors may read it.

Browse the 56 original skins across classic, glitter, music, emo, summer and other collections. Use the Skin Studio when you want to try your own colours and banner. The gallery is open to visitors, but saving a custom design requires an account.

4. Make friends and share the Luv

Visit the Friends section to discover other profiles and send a friend request. Once accepted, your friend connections can appear on your profile. Top 16 lets you highlight favourites, a nod to the original Bebo tradition. The Daily Luv feature has server-side limits to stop one person giving unlimited Luv in a day.

Be friendly with messages and comments. Private Bebo Mail is for the sender and recipient, but you should still avoid sending financial details, passwords or other secrets.

5. Share photos, blogs and videos safely

Build a photo album, add a caption and choose your images. Each album allows up to 96 photos, with a maximum upload size of 5 MB per image. New album photos await moderator approval before they appear in public galleries. A direct storage link may still be accessible if someone obtains it, so do not upload private or sensitive pictures.

On Bebo Videos, members can submit short MP4 or WebM clips under the current upload limits. These are privately held for moderator review before appearing on the public video feed. Share your own recordings or footage you have the right to use. Blog posts and comments may be publicly readable immediately and are not the same as private messages.

6. Stay safe, report and block

If someone is harassing you, open their profile to use Block and Report. Use Report next to wall comments, approved photos and videos where it appears. Moderators can act on reports, hide media and restrict members, but a small developing community may not respond instantly. If you're in immediate danger, contact local authorities.

Read the Community Rules and Privacy Policy before sharing something personal.

7. Help shape the new community

The classic Bebo feeling is built from community friendships, colourful profiles and small personal touches — not just a familiar name. Try different features and tell us which experiences you want improved. Use Suggestions when signed in to report a bug or suggest something new.

Remember: Bebo.nz is independent and not affiliated with the owners or operators of the old Bebo website. None of the original Bebo accounts, photos or messages have been transferred.
