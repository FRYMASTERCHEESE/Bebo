Frequently asked questions about Bebo.nz

Is this the original Bebo website?

No. Bebo.nz is a newly created, independent fan-made social website inspired by the profile styles of the mid-2000s. We are not affiliated with the original Bebo business or its owners. The old database, memberships and lost photos have not been restored.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. There is no membership subscription required to create a Bebo.nz account. Optional advertising might be introduced later to help with hosting costs, but advertising is not enabled at the time this page was published.

Can I find old friends?

You can browse profiles of people who have joined this new community and send them new friend requests. Your old Bebo friends list is not available unless those people independently register here.

Can I use Bebo on Android or iPhone?

Yes, Bebo.nz runs in a browser on phones, tablets and computers. The header has Sign In and Sign Up for guests and Log Out for signed-in members. Horizontal navigation on small screens can be swiped sideways to reach more features.

Why aren't my pictures or videos visible yet?

Uploaded videos and album photos need moderator approval before being listed publicly. The reviewer can approve or reject an upload. This helps prevent unacceptable media appearing in public feeds; it does not mean uploads are confidential or guaranteed to be approved within a fixed time.

I can't log in — what do I do?

Use Sign In and the Forgot Password option. Open the recovery email on the same phone or computer and choose a fresh password. Do not share recovery links with another person. If email confirmation is still pending, follow the original confirmation message rather than creating duplicate accounts.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. The Account section includes a permanent deletion request with a password check and explicit confirmation. This is a destructive step: back up any photos or writing you wish to keep first. Provider logs and backups may retain some operational records temporarily.

How do I report abusive content?

Use the Report option on member profiles, wall comments, approved photos or public videos. For safety concerns without a dedicated Report button, sign in and use Suggestions. Do not wait for a website moderator if there is an emergency.

Will Bebo.nz be showing advertising?

The operator is preparing the site for a possible Google AdSense application. No ad code has been enabled as part of this preparation. See Privacy and Advertising for how ad data could be handled if advertising is enabled and consent controls are configured.

What if I find a bug?

Share details such as which feature you used, your device type and the message shown, without including passwords or private tokens. Use Suggestions. Reports help prioritise fixes while the community grows.
