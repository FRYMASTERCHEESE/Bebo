Privacy & advertising policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

At a glance: Bebo.nz is an independently operated, developing community for adults aged 18 or older. It uses Supabase for accounts, database and media storage and GitHub Pages for its public website. Information you publish in public profiles, comments and approved media can be seen by other visitors. Google advertising is not enabled on this website at the time of this update.

Who operates Bebo.nz?

Bebo.nz is an independent fan-made social project, not connected with the historical Bebo company or its former owners. See our About page for project identity and technical information. Questions and privacy concerns can be sent to the site team through Suggestions after signing into your account. Do not include passwords or private identification documents in a suggestion.

What information is used?

  • Account details: Your sign-up email and authentication information are handled by Supabase Auth. You choose your own password; the website does not ask you to publish it. Confirmation and password reset emails may be delivered by Supabase and configured email services.
  • Member-created information: Username, display name, status, About Me details, profile images, custom skins, albums, blogs, friendships, whiteboards, groups, polls, videos and comments, depending on what you choose to share.
  • Private services: Private messages, blocked members, moderation reports and account requests are stored with access controls. Reported material may be viewed by authorised moderators for safety investigations.
  • Technical data: Authentication sessions, necessary browser storage, request timestamps and technical connection data such as IP addresses may be processed by hosting, authentication and media-delivery providers for security and operation.

Which information can other people see?

Public usernames, profiles, wall comments, blog content, albums, approved photos, approved videos and other public features may appear to visitors, search engines or people who share a link. Other members may be able to see content you publish in groups or on profile walls. Do not publish someone else's private details.

Bebo Mail is intended for the sender and recipient, subject to platform access protections. Uploaded videos require moderator approval before appearing publicly. Newly uploaded album photos also require review before they are listed in public galleries. Photo storage uses publicly addressable object URLs; someone who obtains such a direct URL may access it before review, so do not upload sensitive or confidential photographs.

Storage, cookies and local preferences

The website saves a preference in your browser for the 2005 or 2007 Bebo appearance. Supabase Auth uses browser/session storage to keep you signed in. We use necessary functionality to authenticate accounts, prevent abuse, save profile changes and serve requested files. The hosting and embedded providers may process technical connection data when you use their services. A YouTube video embedded by a member may involve Google's own playback and privacy terms.

Advertising: not switched on yet

Bebo.nz may apply to display advertising through Google AdSense in future. This policy describes what could change if ads are actually enabled; it does not mean advertising tracking is running now. If enabled, Google and other approved advertising partners may process cookies, IP addresses, web beacons or similar identifiers to deliver and measure ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on previous visits to this website or other websites. Google's advertising cookies may help Google and its partners deliver ads based on those visits.

You can learn how Google uses data on partner sites and manage personalised advertising at Google My Ad Center. You may be able to opt out of some other vendors through industry opt-out choices.

Before enabling advertising, we will need to update the actual list of ad providers, confirm that this policy matches deployed scripts and configure appropriate privacy choices. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland must be offered the required certified consent-management experience before personalised Google ads are served there. Ads will not be inserted into sign-in, password-recovery, admin, moderation or private-message interfaces as part of the current preparation work.

Why data is used and shared

We use information to run accounts, deliver social features, apply user-selected preferences, maintain security, respond to reports and protect members. Relevant data is processed by infrastructure providers including GitHub Pages and Supabase to provide these services. Information that you intentionally publish may be seen by the public. We do not publish an account's password or private messages as public website content.

Access, corrections, deletion and retention

Members can edit their profiles, remove their own eligible posts or media, block other members and use the account-deletion process under Account. Permanent deletion asks for your current password and explicit confirmation. Account data and linked media are scheduled for removal by the existing Bebo deletion process, but operational logs and provider backups may be retained for limited periods according to the services' policies. We have not yet published a fully verified fixed retention schedule or independently tested every deletion case; please do not assume immediate removal from all caches or backups.

Request corrections or ask questions using the signed-in Suggestions and Feedback page. If you cannot access your account, use the password reset process. For urgent threats or illegal activity, contact local authorities as well as reporting the content in Bebo.

Updates to this policy

We may update this page as the community grows or when advertising, support or retention practices change. The update date appears above. This notice explains our current implementation and intended changes; it does not replace independent legal advice about New Zealand or international privacy obligations.
