Community guidelines & moderation

Updated: 11 October 2026

Bebo.nz is an independent, adult-only (18+) social community. Respect people, get permission before sharing their information or media, and report harmful content. These rules apply to profiles, posts, photos, videos, blogs, comments, group names, messages and external links.

What belongs on Bebo.nz?

Original photos you may share, friendly comments, genuine updates, personal blogs, drawings, creative skins, community discussions, music interests and memories of the 2000s. Keep your profile your own and enjoy the classic Bebo experience without impersonating its historical users or the original company.

What isn't allowed?

  • Abuse: Bullying, hate speech, stalking, threats, intimidation, unwanted sexual content or harassment.
  • Privacy invasions: Sharing addresses, private phone numbers, personal documents, passwords, intimate images or another person's private messages without permission.
  • Harmful or illegal material: Exploitation, content involving minors in sexual situations, dangerous threats, unlawful sales, malware, scams or fraud.
  • Copyright and impersonation: Uploading pictures or videos you have no right to publish; pretending to be another person, organisation or the original Bebo service.
  • Spam and manipulation: Repetitive promotions, unwanted mass messaging, fake engagement, attempts to bypass moderation or misleading links.

How photos and videos are reviewed

Videos: Newly submitted videos enter a pending state and are not listed for public playback until an authorised moderator approves them. Members can report published videos; moderators can remove them from the public feed.

Album photos: New uploaded photo records also start pending, not in the public gallery, until an authorised moderator approves them. Approved photos can be reported and hidden. The original upload files are held in a publicly addressable storage bucket; a direct object URL can still be shared. Do not upload sensitive private material, even for pending review.

Other posts: Wall comments and profiles can be reported and reviewed by the owner/admin team. Members can now report blog entries, blog comments and group descriptions; authorised moderators can hide this content from public lists without deleting the owner's original records. These text posts are not pre-approved, and other profile content, polls and quizzes still need ongoing oversight. Moderation cannot guarantee every public item complies with policy at all times.

How to report a concern

  1. On a profile or wall comment, use Report. On approved videos use Report, and on approved album photos use Report photo (requires sign-in).
  2. Explain the concern clearly, without repeating the harmful content unnecessarily. Moderators may review relevant material and the reason for the report.
  3. Where necessary, use Block on a member's profile. Blocking limits interactions but does not make public information private.
  4. On blog pages, blog comments and the Groups page, use their new Report buttons when signed in. For other concerns without a Report button, send a signed-in suggestion marked as a safety issue. For immediate danger or serious criminal activity, contact local authorities.

What moderators can do

Approved moderators have a protected Admin Panel for reviewing wall/profile reports, a separate video-review area for pending and reported video clips, and a photo-review queue for pending and reported pictures. They may remove comments, hide or reject media, warn or suspend members and mark reports reviewed. Suspended accounts may still access public-reading features, but posting is restricted server-side.

Moderation decisions are based on the context available. Not every report means a rule was broken. Decisions may take time, particularly while this is a small developing community; there is no guaranteed response time or 24-hour monitoring promise.

Appeals, copyright and privacy requests

If you believe a moderation decision is wrong, or your own photo or content was used without permission, submit a specific request via Suggestions and Feedback while signed in. Describe the page or account involved and why you disagree. Do not include authentication tokens or identification documents. A private, independent contact channel for members who cannot sign in is still being established.

Protect your Bebo account

Use a strong, unique password, check that the website address is https://bebo.nz/, and use Sign Out on shared devices. Never send payment to anyone promising an original historical Bebo login: old accounts and posts have not been imported.
